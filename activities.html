Activities and Experiences

Make your Scottish journey memorable with outdoor adventures, wildlife encounters and beautiful scenery.

Things to Do in Scotland

Find experiences to suit your interests.

Hiking and Walking

Explore mountain trails, peaceful glens and scenic walking routes. Check weather conditions and trail difficulty before setting out.

Wildlife and Nature

Look out for red deer, seabirds and other Scottish wildlife. Observe animals from a respectful distance and protect their natural habitats.

Sightseeing and Photography

Capture historic castles, dramatic landscapes, coastal views and charming villages throughout Scotland.

Plan a Safe Adventure

Prepare for changing weather, carry suitable clothing and water, and check local guidance before outdoor activities. Respect local communities, wildlife and the natural environment.
