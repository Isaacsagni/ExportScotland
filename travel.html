Your Scotland Travel Guide

Helpful information to plan your journey and enjoy your time exploring Scotland.

Plan Your Trip

A few things to consider before setting off.

Getting Around

Scotland can be explored by train, bus, car or bicycle. Plan routes in advance, especially when visiting rural areas.

Weather and Clothing

Scottish weather can change quickly. Bring waterproof clothing and layers, even when the day starts sunny.

Planning Your Route

Check distances, transport times and opening hours. Allow extra time for scenic stops and outdoor activities.

Travel Tips

Book popular accommodation and attractions ahead during busy seasons. Check local travel information before setting off, respect the natural environment, and follow safety advice when walking in the Highlands.
